How to Use and Format Specifications

How to convert PowerPoint to PDF locally

  1. Select or drop your PowerPoint .pptx presentation into the staging workspace.
  2. Confirm PDF is selected as the output format in the staged item controls.
  3. Click Convert to render each slide with visual fidelity and download your publication-ready PDF.

Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Embedded animations, slide transitions, and embedded audio/video clips are rendered as fixed high-resolution visual slide frames.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

Does the converted PDF keep the exact slide layout and background?

Yes. FormatFoundry uses an advanced browser slide renderer to reproduce master slide backgrounds, color themes, tables, custom shapes, and images identically.

Are my presentation slides uploaded to any remote server?

No. All parsing, slide DOM construction, and PDF compilation execute 100% locally in your browser sandbox.
