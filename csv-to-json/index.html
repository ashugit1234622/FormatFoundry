How to Use and Format Specifications

How to convert CSV to JSON locally

  1. Drop your CSV file into the conversion workspace.
  2. Confirm JSON is selected as the output format.
  3. Click Convert to parse and download your structured JSON document.

Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: All cell values are preserved strictly as strings to prevent unintended coercion of leading-zero identifiers (like postal codes or phone numbers).

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

Does this converter change numbers or dates automatically?

No. FormatFoundry strictly preserves cell values as strings to avoid corrupting leading zeros in account numbers, phone numbers, or formatted codes.

Does it support CSV files saved from Microsoft Excel with a UTF-8 BOM?

Yes. FormatFoundry strips UTF-8 Byte Order Marks automatically without corrupting the first column header name.
