Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Converting a lossy JPG to PNG will not restore compression artifacts that were already present in the original JPEG file, though it prevents any further quality loss.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

Will converting JPG to PNG improve image quality?

No. PNG is a lossless format, so it will preserve the exact pixels of the source JPEG without adding further artifacts, but it cannot reverse lossy compression already baked into the JPEG.

Does this converter accept both .jpg and .jpeg extensions?

Yes. FormatFoundry handles .jpg and .jpeg files identically, verifying the actual JFIF/EXIF binary signature to ensure valid image data.
