How to Use and Format Specifications

How to convert JPG to PowerPoint locally

  1. Drop your JPG photo or diagram into the upload area.
  2. Confirm PowerPoint (.pptx) is selected as the target.
  3. Click Convert to download a PowerPoint presentation instantly.

Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Images exceeding slide dimensions are scaled down proportionally to ensure complete visibility within slide margins.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

Does this compress my original JPG photo?

No. The image is placed directly on the slide at original resolution.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Slide compilation runs completely in your local browser memory.
