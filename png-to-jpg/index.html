How to Use and Format Specifications

How to convert PNG to JPG locally

  1. Select or drag and drop one or more PNG files into the upload area.
  2. Confirm JPG is selected as the target format in the staged item controls.
  3. Click Convert to rasterize locally and download the resulting JPG file or a ZIP archive.

Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Because the JPEG format does not support alpha transparency channels, transparent areas in your PNG images are automatically composited onto a solid white background.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to transparent pixels when converting PNG to JPG?

JPEG does not support alpha transparency. FormatFoundry automatically composites any transparent areas onto a clean, solid white background matte before encoding.

Are my PNG images uploaded to an external server?

No. All file reading, decoding, canvas compositing, and JPEG encoding execute strictly in your local browser memory using JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas.

Can I batch convert multiple PNG images to JPG at once?

Yes. You can drag and drop multiple PNG files into the workspace, click Convert All, and download each result individually or bundle them together into a single ZIP archive.
