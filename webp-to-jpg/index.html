How to convert WebP to JPG locally

  2. Ensure JPG is selected in the format picker.

Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Any alpha transparency present in the source WebP is composited onto a solid white background because the JPEG standard does not support alpha channels.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

Why convert WebP to JPG?

While WebP is widely supported in modern web browsers, some older desktop applications, email clients, and printing services require standard JPEG files.
