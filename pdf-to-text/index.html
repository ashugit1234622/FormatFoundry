How to Use and Format Specifications

How to convert PDF to Text locally

  1. Select or drag and drop your PDF document into the staging area.
  2. Confirm Plain Text (.txt) is selected as your conversion target.
  3. Click Convert to extract all page text and download the resulting TXT file.

Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Scanned image-only PDFs containing raster scans rather than selectable text require optical character recognition (OCR), which is not performed by this purely structural extractor.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool upload my PDF to any server?

No. All PDF parsing and text stream decoding execute 100% locally in your browser memory.

Can I extract text from scanned PDFs?

This tool extracts digital text embedded in the PDF document. If the PDF consists solely of scanned image snapshots, OCR processing would be needed.
