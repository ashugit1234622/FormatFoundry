How to Use and Format Specifications

How to convert Text to PowerPoint locally

  1. Drop your plain text file into the converter area.
  2. Select PowerPoint (.pptx) as the output format.
  3. Click Convert to generate a formatted PowerPoint slide deck.

Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Paragraphs separated by blank lines become individual slide cards; keep text concise for best visual presentation.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

How does FormatFoundry divide text into slides?

Double newlines separate text sections into distinct slides, generating an introductory title slide followed by organized content cards.

Is my presentation outline uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is constructed inside your browser session.
