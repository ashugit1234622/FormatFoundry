Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Transparent backgrounds in vector graphics are filled with solid white (#FFFFFF) because JPEG does not support alpha channels.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

What color is used for transparent SVG areas?

Because JPEG does not support transparency, transparent vector backgrounds are composited onto a solid white background (#FFFFFF).
