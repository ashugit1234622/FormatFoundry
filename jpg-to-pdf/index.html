How to Use and Format Specifications

How to convert JPG to PDF locally

  1. Select or drop your JPG files into the workspace.
  2. Choose PDF Document as the conversion target.
  3. Click Convert to produce a downloadable PDF file immediately.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting JPG to PDF recompress or reduce photo quality?

No. The original JPEG binary stream is embedded directly into the PDF structure to prevent generational compression loss.

Can I convert scanned receipts and forms to PDF?

Yes. JPG scans of documents, receipts, and photos convert cleanly into standard printable PDF files.
