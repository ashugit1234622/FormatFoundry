How to Use and Format Specifications

How to convert PNG to PDF locally

  1. Select or drag PNG images into the staging zone.
  2. Verify PDF Document is chosen as the target format.
  3. Click Convert to compile the image into a clean PDF document.

Technical specifications & limitations

Important format notes: Transparent pixels in PNG files are rendered over standard white PDF document pages.

Client-side privacy guarantee

FormatFoundry executes this conversion entirely within your browser runtime using standard Web APIs. No document, image bytes, metadata, or telemetry are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

How is the image sized on the PDF page?

Images are placed onto standard A4 pages and scaled proportionally to fit within margins while preserving natural aspect ratios.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All PDF compilation is performed client-side using JavaScript in your browser.
